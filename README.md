## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, immigration forms, and meteorological tracking.

- [Visit Mexico (Official Tourism Portal)](https://visitmexico.com/en/) - Official national tourism board portal featuring destination highlights, travel itineraries, and cultural guides.
- [National Migration Institute (INM)](https://www.gob.mx/inm) - Official government portal for tourist entry regulations, visa requirements, and Digital Multiple Migratory Forms (FMMD).
- [Secretariat of Culture - Archaeological Zones](https://www.gob.mx/cultura) - Official federal heritage portal managing Mexico's major archaeological sites, including Chichén Itzá, Teotihuacán, and Palenque.
- [SMN Mexico Weather (Servicio Meteorológico Nacional)](https://smn.conagua.gob.mx/) - Official national weather service tracking tropical storms, hurricanes, and regional forecasts.

## Transportation

Navigating intercity bus systems, domestic airlines, regional rail networks, and public transit.

- [ADO Bus Lines](https://www.ado.com.mx/) - Major long-distance intercity coach network connecting Mexico City, Puebla, Oaxaca, and the entire Yucatán Peninsula.
- [Tren Maya Official Portal](https://www.visitmexico.com/en/reservaciones) - Official railway passenger service linking cultural hubs and archaeological sites across southeastern Mexico.
- [Aeroméxico](https://www.aeromexico.com/) - National flag carrier operating extensive domestic flight routes across all major Mexican airports.
- [Volaris](https://www.volaris.com/) - Major domestic low-cost airline providing high-frequency connections across primary and secondary cities.

## Destinations & Regions

### Mexico City & Central Plateau

- [Mexico City Official Visitor Guide](https://mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/?lang=en) - Municipal tourism portal detailing Centro Histórico walking routes, Chapultepec, museums, and public transit apps.
- [Mexico City Integrated Transit (CDMX Mobility)](https://mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/?lang=en) - Official guide for navigating the Metro, Metrobús, Trolebús, and Ecobici bike-share network.

### Yucatán Peninsula & Mayan Riviera

- [Quintana Roo Tourism Portal](https://qroo.gob.mx/) - Regional travel hub covering Cancún, Playa del Carmen, Tulum, Cozumel, and the Caribbean coast.
- [Yucatán Tourism Board](https://yucatan.travel/) - Official visitor guide for Mérida, colonial yellow towns (Pueblos Mágicos), cenotes, and Mayan ruins.

### Oaxaca & Southern Highlands

- [Oaxaca State Tourism Portal](https://www.oaxaca.gob.mx/) - Official regional guide for exploring indigenous markets, mezcal routes, Monte Albán, and traditional culinary arts.

### Baja California & Pacific Coast

- [Baja California Sur Tourism](https://www.bcs.gob.mx/) - Official travel planner for Los Cabos, La Paz, whale watching lagoons, and Pacific coastal deserts.
- [Puerto Vallarta Tourism Board](https://visitpuertovallarta.com/) - Visitor guide for Banderas Bay beaches, the historic Malecón, and surrounding mountain villages.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers, remote professionals, and digital nomads in Mexico.

- [Temporary Resident Visa (SRE Consular Services)](https://sre.gob.mx/) - Official government guidelines for obtaining temporary residency visas for remote workers and freelancers staying over 180 days.
- [Mexico City Digital Nomad Community Hubs](https://mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/?lang=en) - Neighborhood coworking and coliving resources across vibrant districts like Roma, Condesa, and Polanco.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contact lines, roadside assistance, and safety precautions.

- [Green Angels (Ángeles Verdes)](https://visitmexico.com/en/) - Official government roadside assistance fleet providing mechanical help, tourist information, and emergency support on federal highways (Dial 078).
- [Emergency Assistance Hotline (911)](https://www.gob.mx/) - Direct national emergency hotline for police, fire, and medical dispatch across all Mexican states.
